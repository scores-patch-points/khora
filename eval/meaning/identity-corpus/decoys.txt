Decoys, planted. Four people who share a surname with someone in the Motorola resolution or the council debate and are not them: they live in other stories, and nothing here is about Nashville, Motorola or the Council.

Sarah Rosenberg is a cardiologist in Duluth. Sarah Rosenberg treats patients with arrhythmia. Rosenberg published a study of beta blockers last winter. Sarah Rosenberg teaches residents at the university hospital. Rosenberg prescribes statins to older patients. The hospital hired Sarah Rosenberg ten years ago. Rosenberg reviews trials for a medical journal. Sarah Rosenberg also runs a free clinic on Saturdays.

Tom Allen is a left fielder for the Rapids. Tom Allen hit thirty home runs last season. Allen signed a contract with the club in March. Tom Allen trains with a batting coach every morning. Allen stole forty bases in his second year. The Rapids traded Tom Allen to a rival team. Allen broke his wrist during spring training. Tom Allen returned to the lineup in June.

Mary Butler is a violinist with the Lakeshore Orchestra. Mary Butler plays Brahms and Bartok. Butler recorded a concerto with a Swedish conductor. Mary Butler gives lessons to young students. Butler toured Japan with a string quartet. The orchestra promoted Mary Butler to concertmaster. Butler owns a violin made in Cremona. Mary Butler performs every December at the cathedral.

James Reed is a baker in Lyon. James Reed bakes rye bread and croissants. Reed opened his bakery after working for a famous pastry chef. James Reed delivers bread to local restaurants. Reed wins prizes at the regional baking fair. The bakery hired James Reed's cousin as an apprentice. Reed buys flour from a mill near the river. James Reed closes the shop on Mondays.
