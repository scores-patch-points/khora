# Equality, falsified against what we already hold (2026-10-26)

**The assertion (the operator):** *two things are equal if the identity on both sides of the equation cancel each other out when viewed from the same perspective, with a margin of error smaller than a DMD.*
**The brief:** try to break it with past research, the financial work above all. Read-only: no production code changed. Script: `eval/meaning/equality-falsify.mjs`; the financial data is `EO-terminal` (grounding/corporate-actions.json, core/claimcheck.mjs, company-identity.mjs, holographic-identity.mjs).

## Verdict in one line
It **survives where the financial data tests it**, and it is **incomplete in four places**: (1) "smaller than a DMD" is only the upper bound, the margin also has a floor; (2) a margin makes a compatibility relation, not an equality (it is not transitive); (3) for sums the margin propagates, a single DMD is wrong; (4) "the same perspective" has dimensions the checker does not yet hold (unit and currency, direction, sense, key). Each below, with its evidence and what it does to the definition.

## What SURVIVED
**S1 — the margin plateau (real data, 7 witnessed stock splits).** The SEC share counts give an observed ratio for each split; the code snaps it to a nominal factor within a hand 5% (`snapRatio`). Observed deviation from nominal: 0.13% to 0.70% (AAPL 7:1 seen as 6.951; KO 2:1 as 1.993; AMZN 20:1 as 20.026). The smallest gap between two distinct factors the code can choose is 12.5% (1:8 against 1:7). Result over a ladder of margins: below 0.7% it misses real splits (0.1%: 0 of 7), from 1% to 10% every one snaps to the right factor and none is ambiguous, from 15% two factors fall in one margin. The 5% in the code sits on a plateau of about (0.7%, 12.5%) that the data itself bounds. Margin smaller than a DMD, and the answer does not move across the whole plateau: the equation holds as stated.
**S2 — perspective decides (company-identity.mjs).** The same pair (Google, Alphabet) is "contradicted" for the legal-filer judge (one EIN each) and not for the consolidated-group judge. "Viewed from the same perspective" is not decoration; it changes the verdict, and the code carries it as a for-whom.
**S3 — the 2% rule really is wrong where precision is stated (production code).** `refMeet` calls `$250.0 million` and `$255.0 million` the same, a $5,000,000 difference between two amounts each stated to the nearest $50,000. The stated-precision margin (half the last written place, per side) says they differ. This is the equation's own margin doing better than the hand constant. (The code's comment says "5% when approximate"; the code never does it: comment and code had drifted.)

## What BROKE or was INCOMPLETE
**B1 — the margin has a floor as well as a ceiling.** "Smaller than a DMD" alone admits margin 0, and 0.1% (smaller than any DMD) failed 7 of 7 real splits. The margin must reach the measurement noise (here 0.7%), and the noise must itself be under the DMD. Where the noise is not below the DMD, equality is **undecidable**, and the right answer is a typed gap, not a guess. *Definition becomes:* noise <= margin < DMD, else no verdict.
**B2 — a margin gives compatibility, not equality (it is not transitive).** Through the production meeting (`figMeet`): 100 ~ 101.9 and 101.9 ~ 103.8, but 100 is not ~ 103.8. The stated-precision rule fails the same way by construction (2,445 and 2,435, each to the unit, both fit "2,440" stated to the ten). Janus's own finding agrees (identity README, attack finding 3: "a lens may not be an identity relation"; the Ship-of-Theseus fission is reported, never ranked) and EO-terminal's holographic-identity carries a transitivity check "that names fission". An equation is an equivalence; "within a margin" is not. *Definition becomes:* the margin yields a relation that is reflexive and symmetric but never chained; equality as an equivalence needs both sides taken to the coarser common resolution.
**B3 — for sums the margin propagates; one DMD is the wrong size.** The financial claim-checker tests "rows sum to total, within rounding" with a flat `tol = 1` (checkTableSums). Simulation (synthetic, honestly rounded tables, 20,000 per size): the share of CORRECT tables that rule flags is 0% at 2-3 rows, 1.8% at 5, 6.7% at 8, 13.3% at 12, 24.6% at 20, 34.6% at 30. The error is up to (n+1)/2 units and typically sqrt((n+1)/12). *Definition becomes:* the margin of a derived equality is the propagated resolution of everything that went into it; a total is "equal" to its rows within the sum of their half-places, not within one.
**B4 — value cancelling is necessary, not sufficient: the perspective has dimensions.**
- **Currency and unit are identity.** `$4.0 million`, `4.0 million shares` and `4.0 million millimetres tall` all become `measure: count, base: 4000000`; production `refMeet($4.0 million, 4.0 million shares)` is true. Money has no measure class at all.
- **Direction is identity.** In the financial pairs (relations 502, 503, 509, 521) the amounts are equal ($4.0M, $10.0M, $50.0M, $250.0M) and the facts are opposite (we paid / we received). Same value, opposite sign. From one perspective (ours) a payment out and a payment in do not cancel into "equal"; the stage has a `direction_swap` gap and no direction in its perspective. The equation predicts exactly this, once direction is part of the perspective: it is a prediction the checker cannot yet make.
- **Shared structure is not sameness** (janus identity README, finding 1: two invoices from one template are not a contradiction; identity keys must be declared). Equal numbers under one template are not one thing. In the 108 pairs, contradiction 813 meets on value alone ("30 years" ~ "30 years": the window is equal, the stated odds are not).
- **No difference is not equality** (company-identity.mjs): a pair is only contradicted by a given one-valued relation conflicting on witnessed assertions; with no positive evidence the verdict "is never BOUND". The absence of a difference that matters (the cancelling) does not produce equality by itself; positive evidence is a separate reading.
- Also in the financial work: a figure in a filing lives at a terrain (a line, a total); two statements are equal only after a named lift (janus finding 2), which is B3's propagation from the other side.

## The assertion, as it now reads (a proposal for the operator to dispose)
> Two things are **compatible** from perspective W when, along every dimension W declares (kind and unit, currency, direction, period and sense, declared key), their identities cancel, and the residual of their magnitudes is within the **stated resolution** of the two (propagated through anything summed), that resolution being **at least the measurement noise and under a DMD**. Where the noise reaches the DMD the verdict is a typed gap. Compatibility is never chained: it becomes **equality** only when both sides are taken to the coarser common resolution under the same W.
