**Expected failure modes, stated now.** P2' is the likely miss: dev v5 sat exactly at 0.45 and this exam has more descriptions with a head noun that is not a word of the name (6) and three section openers, which the rules turn to gaps. The weakest attended case is w06 ("It is knocked over in ... Independence Day" after a sentence naming a film). Mary Rose has no attended "It" (the article says "she" or "the ship"). Traps: u04 (cofferdam, previous sentence also names the dam), u16 ("The aircraft" in a Tu-144 section, the same head noun as the topic), u13 ("The monument" for the national monument that includes the statue) and u15 (replica, whose name contains the topic's) are the likeliest wrong binds.
