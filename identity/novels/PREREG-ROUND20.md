# PREREG-ROUND20 — a real-referent gate for he/she, then collapse

The user's directions: "pronouns do need to collapse", and "anaphora doesn't really make sense without a real referent". Observed first (2026-11-18, not a result): on three toy texts the male character's name stands only at sentence starts, is heard but not admitted to the cast, and "he" then binds to the only admitted being, a woman (Mary, Anna, Helen). Today bound pronouns sit beside the cast (`pronounsPresence`), never inside a being or an edge (0 of 94 edges on a Great Expectations excerpt had a pronoun end).

## Part A — the gender of a being from its own words (a measured table)
Table (`scripts/build-name-gender.mjs`, UD English GUM TRAIN gold): for every gold entity with a proper-noun mention, its gender = the gender (Masc / Fem) the entity's third-person singular personal pronouns carry (the majority; entities with none or a tie are skipped); each proper-noun word of its mentions is counted [masc, fem] once per mention. Rule: a being's gender is known when, summed over the words of its mention spans the table holds, one gender has strictly more than the other and the total is at least 2 (the same declared minimum of two observations as the name table); else unknown. The rung receives it as `priorGender` (a fallback behind the rung's own clause-local evidence): a clean he/she then does not bind a being of the other known gender.
- S11 = S0 + the round-14 name ruling (S6) + the name-gender prior. Compared with S0 and S6 on U (66 GUM train documents in no slice; the table built WITHOUT them), pooled, document-cluster bootstrap (as rounds 14-15). U has been scored for S0 and S6 (sighted for those; S11 is new).
- H1: precision(S11) - precision(S6): 95% interval above zero. H2: coverage(S11) - coverage(S6): interval includes zero or is above. H3 (change set): the bindings the gender prior changes are wrong more often than a random set of the same size of S6's bindings (permutation, p < 0.05). H4 (falsifier): tables with the gender labels permuted across words (20 seeds): none reaches the observed precision change. Expectation, stated now: small, because only ~1.5% of English errors were the wrong gender (NOVELS-RESULTS-ROUND10); a null is a result.

## Part B — the real-referent rule and collapse (implemented and tested, then measured descriptively)
- A pronoun binds only to a being that is admitted (cast) or heard with positive evidence of being a person (the name table says person, not only the absence of non-person evidence); otherwise it stays unbound with the typed gap `no_real_referent`.
- Collapse: a bound pronoun becomes a mention of its being (`pronounSpans`, with the binding's mechanism and margin, retractable), and a collapsed text (each bound pronoun replaced by the being's own surface) is read again: the edges that exist only in the collapsed read are the pronoun-led clauses. Reported without a bar on U: extra edges, beings that gain them, and the share of extra edges whose pronoun binding was right (from the gold). No downstream claim beyond that.
Failures reported. No threshold tuned.
