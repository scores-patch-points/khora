# PREREG round 13: read in the cube's dependency order (2026-11-17, before any code of this round)
The 27 phase posts (native/kernel/cube.js) put SEG before CON and DEF before binding; our reader binds pronouns first and parses clauses after. Round 12 decomposed the English default's 31% error: 69% he/she bound to a non-person, 25% a more salient candidate won, 5% the antecedent had not been mentioned yet, 1.5% the other gender; 51% of answerable pronouns are left unbound.
## The stages (each added to the one before, measured alone)
S0 the canonical default read (the presence rung, control dials, no non-personal ruling). S1 DEF·Figure / NUL·Figure: a PERSONHOOD check: a being is vetoed as the referent of he/she when the naive-Bayes evidence of the predicates it is the subject or object of says non-person; the evidence is a measured prior
(P(person | predicate lemma, role) counted from UD English GUM TRAIN with its gold entity types, nsubj and obj of verbs; equal class priors; a being with fewer than 2 scored mentions is not vetoed: a giver may be silent, the reader is not told a thing is not a person for lack of an answer).
S2 SEG·Figure before CON·Figure: the sentence's clauses are parsed first and the pronoun is resolved at SENTENCE CLOSE: candidates are the presence state before the sentence plus the mentions of this sentence (later ones too: cataphora), a mention that is a co-argument of a non-reflexive pronoun in the same clause is excluded and a reflexive binds only to the clause subject; mention weights by role (subject: the boost dial, else 1); same dials as S0.
S3 REC·Figure: a pronoun that cleared no floor or margin is PENDING and carries its candidates; within the next `window` sentences (the presence window dial, 4) it is bound to the candidate the continuing text supports (mentions of exactly one candidate, margin by the same dial), recorded with `realizedAt`.
S4 repair: after the document, a being bound to both a masculine and a feminine pronoun keeps the gender of its clean evidence (the majority) and the minority bindings are RETRACTED (back to unbound, recorded with the reason); nothing is deleted from the record.

## Hypotheses (each can fail)
H1 (S1): precision rises over S0 and the coverage lost is no more than the bindings vetoed (interval of the precision gain excludes 0). H2 (S2): over S1, precision or coverage rises with the other not falling by more than the document-level spread (1.4826 x MAD). H3 (S3): late bindings are bound at precision above the permutation null's and above 0.5.
H4 (S4): precision rises over S3 with every retraction recorded. H5 (all): S4 beats S0 on BOTH precision and coverage. H6: the gold-free contradiction rate on the novels falls from S0 to S4 (book-cluster interval excludes 0).
Predictions, so they can fail: S1 raises precision from about 0.69 to about 0.80 at about 10 points of coverage lost; S2 raises coverage by a few points; S3 binds a small share (under 10% of answerable) at precision near 0.6; S4 adds under 2 points of precision; H5 FAILS (coverage stays at or below S0's because S1 refuses bindings); H6 holds.
Not tested: languages other than English; the thoughts/competence effects of the new bindings (a later round); non-he/she pronouns.
