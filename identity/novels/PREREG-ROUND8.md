# PREREG round 8: competence = thoughts about earlier records that would have made them less surprising, recorded by the system into the pharos (2026-11-17, before any code)
Definition used throughout (the user's): a THOUGHT is an entry the system writes about its own earlier records (a wormhole, a synapse, an expressway, or a rule that generates links), citing the places it is about. Thoughts may cite thoughts. COMPETENCE of a thought = the surprise it removes: HINDSIGHT (had it been known from the start, the part of the stream it was proposed from is less surprising, net of its own cost) and FORESIGHT (the later part, which it was never fitted to, is less surprising). Hindsight is the proposal; foresight is the test.
## Stream and reader (competence.mjs)
A book is read as an ordered stream of EVENTS = referent-bearing mentions in reading order: named cast mentions (symbol = the cast referent), third-person singular pronouns of the presence rung's class (symbol "p:he" ...), and descriptions (a clause argument starting the/this/that/these/those that overlaps no cast mention; symbol "d:head", as wormhole.mjs).
SURPRISE of an event = -log2 P(symbol | reader state) before the reader sees it (prequential code length). State: presence per referent (the tracker's decayed presence, window 4; a second, long presence, window 32), symbol counts. P(known symbol s) = (1 - pi) * [ 1/2 * presence share (referents only) + 1/2 * count share ]; a first occurrence (birth) has P = pi, pi = (births + 1) / (events + 2).
The constants (1/2 mixture, windows 4 and 32, the Laplace escape) are DECLARED, not tuned. Base reader = no thoughts.
## Thoughts (each proposed from the first two thirds of the book's sentences only)
- WORMHOLE (description link, identity-tested as in round 6; effect: every occurrence of "the h" becomes a mention of the referent). Cost = log2(number of description heads) + log2(number of referents) bits.
- RULE (the pronoun presence rung's bindings: every pronoun the rung binds becomes a mention of its referent). It is a thought that generates many level-1 links; its cost is the description length of its dials: log2 of the declared grid of eval/referents/PREREG-PRESENCE.md (8 x 3 x 2 x 6 x 6 = 1728 settings) = 10.75 bits, once.
- SYNAPSE and EXPRESSWAY (a pair of referents (i, j): when i is present, j's expectation is raised by w * presence_i, w = P(j appears within the window | i appears) from the training third). Cost = 2 log2(number of referents). Candidates: the pairs whose co-presence credit (window co-count minus the count expected under independence) is above the median plus 1.4826 x MAD of all credits (a typed gap with fewer than 3 candidates). A pair is an EXPRESSWAY when its long-window (32) net saving exceeds its short-window (4) net saving by more than the robust spread of those differences, else a SYNAPSE.
NET saving of a thought = (bits of the first two thirds without it) - (bits with it) - its cost. ADMITTED when net exceeds the median plus 1.4826 x MAD of the net savings of its candidate kind (the kinds with fewer than 3 candidates: when net > 0). Admitted thoughts are applied together.
FORESIGHT saving of a thought = (bits of the last third without it) - (bits with it); the book's COMPETENCE = total foresight saving of the admitted set / bits of the last third.
## Recording (the pharos)
Every admitted thought is woven as an entry (kinds thought-wormhole, thought-rule, thought-synapse, thought-expressway) with its ends, window, hindsight, cost and net, and the byte addresses (EOT source sha256 + UTF-8 byte range) of the places it cites. A thought whose foresight saving is negative is later RETRACTED by a thought-retract entry citing it (nothing is deleted). A META-THOUGHT per kind (thought-meta) cites its instances (`of`) and records their foresight precision and mean saving.
REPLAY: the thought set is rebuilt from the log alone and the last-third bits recomputed: they must equal the originals exactly.
## Data
DEVELOP: the 34 books read before round 6 (all seen). TEST: the 8 round-6 books (never used for thought-competence). The test is the last third of each book (never used to propose or admit a thought) and the code frozen first.
## Hypotheses (each can fail)
K1 (hindsight): by construction admitted thoughts have net > 0 on the first two thirds; reported only.
K2 (foresight): the total foresight saving of the admitted set is above 0 on the test books (book-cluster bootstrap 95% interval excludes 0) and more than half of the admitted thoughts have foresight > 0.
K3 (hindsight predicts foresight): across admitted thoughts, the Spearman correlation of net hindsight saving with foresight saving is above 0 (interval excludes 0).
K4 (kinds): the RULE has the largest total foresight saving; wormholes the smallest; expressways are the rarest kind.
K5 (null): admitted thoughts beat a null of the same number and kind with random referents (real - null foresight saving, interval excludes 0).
K6 (log): replay from the pharos log reproduces every book's last-third bits exactly; retractions are recorded for every thought with negative foresight.
Predictions so they can fail: K2 holds (relative competence 1-5%); K3 holds weakly (rho about 0.2); K4 holds; K5 holds; K6 holds (deterministic). Exploratory: meta-thought precision from earlier books used to set the admission bar of later books (reported, not a hypothesis).
Not tested: any claim beyond this measured surprise reduction; thoughts beyond these four kinds; other modalities.
