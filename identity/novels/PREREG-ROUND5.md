# PREREG round 5: learn how to choose the answer (2026-11-17, before any learner is built and before any final-test question is opened)
Rounds 1-4: localisation works (the named referents place the answer's sentence in ~1.5% of a book, ~30x chance), hand-written choosing rules do not (all at the floor; the apparent leads of round 3 did not replicate). The choosing step is a learning problem with supervision, so the system learns it.
## Data
LEARN/DEVELOP: the 25 books already read (dev 4a92bf27; round-1 eight; round-3 holdout eight; round-4 fresh eight) and their ~385 filtered questions: leave-one-BOOK-out (LOBO) throughout (a book's questions are never in its own training set). FINAL TEST: the next 8 never-read test books of the seeded order
(books-order.json positions 32-39, same integrity rule), read whole through the one reader; their questions filtered and opened only after the learner and its procedure are frozen (FREEZE-ROUND5.md).
## The learner (no LLM; counts and gradient descent only)
For each question, a POOL of candidate spans = the reader's candidates (round-1 definition, amended) in the union of: the referent cut (hop 0 / hop 1, tracked graph), the top 25 sentences of the entity-scene BM25 ordering, the top 10 of plain BM25. Pool recall (an exact-match candidate for a reference answer is in the pool) is reported.
Features per candidate (fixed now): type match; is a referent (cast or tracked); role (subject / object / cast mention / edge end); capitalised; length; overlaps the question's content words; log frequency of the span in the book and inside the cut; the sentence's BM25 score, its rank percentile in the entity-scene ordering, whether it is in the cut / hop 0 / the scene;
the number and IDF-weight of question terms in the sentence; word distance to the nearest question term, and before/after it; is the other end of an act about an active referent, and whether that act's predicate lemma is in the question; first typed candidate of its sentence; number of typed candidates in the sentence; and the question type crossed with (type match, referent, capitalised, preceded by a preposition, has a digit).
Model: listwise softmax regression over a question's pool (target: the candidates whose F1 against a reference answer is >= 0.5), L2, Adam, fixed epochs; features standardised on the training books. Answer = argmax; its softmax mass is the confidence.
## "How to learn" (what the system measures about its own learning)
(a) LEARNING CURVE: LOBO F1 with m = 4, 8, 16, 24 training books (3 random draws each); the slope at the end says whether more data would help. (b) ATTENTION: leave-one-GROUP-out feature ablation (sentence/retrieval, candidate, graph-act, position) in LOBO F1, and the standardised weights of the all-data model. (c) PROGRESS BY QUESTION TYPE (who/what/where/number): the curve per type, so the next data to gather is the type with the steepest slope.
## Hypotheses (can fail)
L1: LOBO F1 of the learned ranker exceeds B1's (book-cluster bootstrap 95%, 10,000, interval excludes 0). L2: the learning curve is still rising at 24 books (F1 at 24 minus F1 at 8 > 0, interval excludes 0). L3: removing the sentence/retrieval group hurts LOBO F1 the most. L4 (final, unread books, one run): the frozen learner (trained on all 25 books) exceeds B1.
Predictions so they can fail: L1 holds weakly (F1 0.10-0.15 against B1 ~0.08); L2 fails or is flat (with ~385 questions the curve saturates early); L3 fails (candidate features matter most, because B1 itself is a candidate-frequency rule); L4 fails or holds only marginally.
Not tested: whether it understands; non-extractive questions. Negative results are reported at the same weight.
