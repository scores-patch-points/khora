# RESULTS round 8: competence = thoughts about earlier records that would have made them less surprising (2026-11-17)
Prereg PREREG-ROUND8.md (19:46:30Z), FREEZE-ROUND8.md (19:49:40Z, before the test books were run). Development: 33 read books (the prereg said 34; one read book has no questions). Test: the 8 round-6 short books. Raw: NOVELS-ROUND8-test-raw.txt, competence-dev.log, competence-test.log; pharos logs in logs8/ (not committed).
Method in one line: each book is a stream of mentions (named, pronoun, description); surprise = -log2 of the reader's prediction before the event; four kinds of thought (wormhole, pronoun rule, synapse, expressway) proposed from the first two thirds and costed; hindsight = surprise removed there, foresight = surprise removed in the last third, which the thought never saw; every admitted thought is written into a pharos log with citations, retracted there if its foresight is negative, summarised by a meta-thought per kind, and the log is replayed.
## Results on the test books (8) / development (33)
- K2 foresight: HELD. Foresight saving 109.7 bits per book [44.8, 194.7] (dev 500.5 [384, 622]); 8 of 8 books positive (dev 33 of 33); 8.06% of last-third surprise removed (dev 10.97%); 63.6% of admitted thoughts have foresight above 0 (dev 59.4%).
- K3 hindsight predicts foresight: dev HELD (Spearman 0.426 [0.303, 0.528], 667 thoughts); test NOT SUPPORTED (0.417 [-0.024, 0.808], 59 thoughts, 8 books).
- K4 kinds: PARTLY. The pronoun rule is the largest (test 814 of 1,048 bits; dev 83% of the total) and wormholes are the smallest on the test books (32 bits), but on development expressways were smaller (250 vs 286) and the prediction that expressways are the rarest kind is FALSE (wormholes are rarer: 23 vs 90 on dev, 3 vs 12 on test).
- K5 against random thoughts of the same number and kind (rule excluded): HELD. real minus null +23.5 bits per book [8.7, 42.8] (dev +109.7 [77, 146]); the random thoughts HURT (null mean -5.0 / -32.9).
- K6 the log: HELD. Replay from the pharos reproduces the last-third bits exactly on 8/8 (33/33) books; a retraction is recorded for every thought with negative foresight on 8/8 (33/33).
By kind, share of admitted thoughts with positive foresight (test / dev): rule 100/100, wormhole 100/96, expressway 92/53, synapse 48/57. Per-thought mean foresight (test): rule 116 bits, wormhole 10.6, expressway 7.0, synapse 2.7.
## What it says
Thoughts the system proposes from the earlier part of a book remove surprise in the later part it never saw, and random thoughts of the same kind do the opposite. Hindsight is a weak-to-moderate guide to foresight (dev 0.43). The retraction record is consistent with the data (every negative thought is retracted) and the state replays exactly from the log, so the thoughts are recorded by the system itself, not reconstructed.
## What to be careful about
- Most of the saving (83%) is the pronoun rule. The base reader has no model of pronouns at all (it codes "he" by frequency), so any thought that says who a pronoun is gains a lot; the number is large partly because the baseline is weak. Without the rule the saving is small: about 18 bits per book on the test books (1.3% of last-third surprise) and 77 on development (1.6%). That smaller figure is the fairer measure of the synapses, expressways and wormholes.
- A thought changes the symbol a mention is coded as (a pronoun becomes a referent), so the comparison is between two descriptions of the same stream, with the declared model, not between two proofs of the same quantity. The mixture weights, windows (4 and 32) and the escape rule are declared, not tuned; a different reader would give different numbers.
- Foresight here is later text of the same book. It does not show that a thought learned on one book helps on another; that is the next test.
- 8 test books, all short; K3's interval includes 0 there. The pheromone-style gate of round 5 is not used here; admission is by net saving against the kind's own spread.
