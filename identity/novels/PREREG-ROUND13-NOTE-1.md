# NOTE 1 to PREREG-ROUND13, 2026-11-17, before any stage was scored: S5, the two pipelines chasing each other
From the asset tracker session's design (pixels earn their presence by independent voices; pixels are ignored only for a named cause; the two chase to a fixpoint; the contested remainder gets the expensive refinement; later frames can overturn earlier calls), applied to pronouns.
S5 (native/adapters/text/pronoun-chase.js): INCLUSION: a candidate being is accepted for a pronoun only if at least k = 2 INDEPENDENT voices vouch for it and it leads the runner-up by at least one vote. The voices, each a different evidence source: SALIENCE (its presence clears the floor), ROLE (it is a subject and so is the pronoun: same-role continuity),
PERSON (its personhood evidence is positive on at least 2 scored arguments), GENDER (its gender, known from a clean bound pronoun, equals the pronoun's), CONTINUITY (the text after the pronoun, within the window, mentions it). EXCLUSION: a candidate is removed only for a NAMED cause, recorded: non_person, gender_mismatch, co_argument (same clause, non-reflexive pronoun),
reflexive_non_subject, out_of_window. THE CHASE: over the whole document, accept every unique leader with >= k votes, add what that teaches (a clean bound he/she fixes the being's gender, which excludes it for the other gender everywhere, earlier and later), recompute, until nothing changes. CONTESTED = a tie at the top or a leader below k votes with no exclusion explaining the rest: reported with
the reason, and given the refinement (CONTINUITY votes are computed only for these, then the chase runs again). The document's contested share is reported; no hand-set failure threshold is declared (to be reported, then derived against the permutation null).
Evaluated as stage S5 beside S0-S4 on GUM dev (development) and, frozen, on GUM test. Hypothesis H7 (can fail): S5 beats S4 on precision AND coverage jointly (not worse on either, better on one), document-cluster interval of the better one excludes 0. Prediction: it holds on precision (about +3 points) and fails on coverage.
k = 2 is a declared structural minimum (one voice is the rung we already have); it is not tuned on the test file.
