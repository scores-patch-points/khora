# PREREG-SYNAPSE-2 (2026-11-17, before any code of this round is written or run): a synapse has two roles, expressway and INS of an induced kind
Direction from the user: "synapses are both expressways and the INS of inducedKinds". Follows PREREG-SYNAPSE-1 (graphs, promotion, dynamics, null unchanged; same 34 weft books, same seeds, same synapses). No LLM.
## What khora stores (read from native/kernel/entity-kind-induction.js, kind-functional-induction.js, cube.js)
INS is the operator in the Existence domain, mode Generate (cube.js): the act that brings an instance into being under a form. An induced kind is a record EOKindCandidate@1: kindKey ("kind:basin:<hash>" for interaction-affinity basins, "kind:cs:<hash>" for characteristic-set kinds), memberRefs, structuralSignatures (the relations the members share), field {internalAffinity, boundaryAffinity, bindingEnergy, stable = beat the random-subset null}, standing "structural_kind_hypothesis" (never "given"). Per kind, each relation gets a standing: unexposed (never tested), refuted, candidate (agreeing where exposed), untested (disagreeing but unwitnessed: not agreement). So the kernel already induces kinds as BASINS of an affinity graph, admitted by a null on internal-minus-boundary affinity, with typed gaps. The synapse basin is the same object with a different affinity (earned co-presence instead of shared relations).
## Two roles, one edge store, two records
EXPRESSWAY = a pairwise edge (i,j,w): makes the walk from i to j one hop. INS = a record over a SET (the basin): `ins(kindKey, memberRefs, evidence = edge ids, bindingEnergy, p, untested = members with no profile)`. One stored synapse edge can be an expressway without any INS (a bridge between kinds) and can be the evidence for an INS; so edge and INS are two records, the INS pointing at edges. Each edge is labelled by derivation: intra (both ends members of one admitted kind), bridge (ends in different kinds), untested (an end has no profile).
## Measurements added
PROFILE of a referent = the set of signatures `subj:<lemma>`, `obj:<lemma>` of weft clauses whose subject/object span overlaps one of the referent's mention spans (relation profile, never the name). Affinity s(x,y) = Jaccard of profile sets. Members with empty profile are UNTESTED, not agreeing.
INDUCED KINDS of a book = kernel characteristicSetKinds(features) (the khora kernel, imported read-only, draws 50, alpha 0.05, seed 1) over these profiles.
ADMISSION of a synapse basin A (size >= 3) as a kind: tested members T = members with a profile (|T| < 3: typed gap); binding(A) = mean s inside T minus mean s between T and the other profiled graph nodes; null = 200 random subsets of the profiled graph nodes of size |T|; admitted when p = (exceeding+1)/201 <= 0.05 (the kernel's alpha).
EXPRESSWAY COST. G_dir = graph of pairs co-present in the SAME sentence in TRAIN (the trail walked hop by hop). Test pairs = pairs of scene members (the 20-sentence TEST blocks of round 1, up to 30 blocks) that are both synapse nodes. d_base = hop distance in G_dir; d_syn = in G_dir plus the synapse edges; d_null = in G_dir plus a degree-preserving rewired copy of the synapses (10 shuffles, mean). A pair is SHORTENED when d falls (a newly connected pair counts as shortened). COMPLETION COST = edge reads of the clamped dynamics (sum over iterations of the degrees of active nodes); efficiency = F1 per 1000 edge reads, real vs RAW vs NULL graph.
## Hypotheses (can fail; sign test across usable books, two-sided exact, alpha 0.05)
E1 expressway: share of test pairs shortened is larger with the synapses than with the rewired null edges.
E1b composition: the same, restricted to test pairs that are not themselves a synapse edge (shortening by composing hops, not by direct hit).
E2 cost: F1 per 1000 edge reads of the synapse graph exceeds the RAW graph's.
K1 kind: the share of basins admitted as kinds by the binding test is larger for the real synapse graph than for rewired null graphs (3 shuffles, basins computed the same way).
K2 homophily: among synapse edges whose two ends both belong to a kernel-induced kind (a book is usable if >= 3 such edges), the share that lie within one induced kind is larger than in the rewired null.
Also reported, no prediction: usable-book counts, shares of intra/bridge/untested edges, d_base of intra vs bridge edges (does the expressway role live apart from the kind role), how many basins have a typed gap.
## Predictions, so they can fail
E1 holds (real edges predict held-out co-presence, null edges do not). E1b FAILS (composition adds little). E2 holds (RAW is dense), noting that RAW's raw F1 was higher. K1 FAILS or is underpowered (sparse profiles; a size-3 basin rarely beats a random subset). K2 is underpowered: most books license no characteristic-set kind, and a verdict is "underpowered" if fewer than 8 books are usable.
Not tested: any claim that an INS record is a correct kind, beyond agreement with relation profiles.
