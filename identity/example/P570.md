## Two things worth noticing

- **The one-valued rule is only a "suggestion constraint" here** (row 6), not mandatory. The current register treats every
  stated rule alike; weighing a suggestion below a mandatory rule is not done yet.
- **Rules carry their own exceptions** (rows 1 and 5): Zaro Ağa and Thomas Parr, whose recorded lifespans break the
  150-year bound, are named as exceptions, so the range rule is not applied to them.

## Reading the three rules the identity check uses

- **subject type constraint**: the rules on this property are about humans (and the other classes listed). That is the *kind*.
- **single-best-value constraint**: a person has one best date of death. Two different ones, in two records being merged, contradict the merge.
- **difference-within-range constraint**: date of death minus date of birth must lie between the minimum and maximum years. That is "you cannot be born after you are dead".

Every rule cites its own statement id in the register (for example `wikidata:P570#<statement id>`), so a verdict can point at the exact rule it used.
