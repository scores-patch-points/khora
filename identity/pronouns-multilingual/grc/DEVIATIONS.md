# Deviations from PREREG-GRC.md (each logged with the time it was made; none was made after a result run)

D1 (2026-11-17, before any result run; register derivation only). The prereg's three-condition filter admitted mostly-neuter forms (αὐτό, τό, τὠυτὸ). The English class never binds `it`: a binder binds a named being. A fourth condition was added to the register derivation:
the form's majority gender (among Masc, Fem, Neut in its third-person tokens) must be Masc or Fem. It is a measured property of the form, not a list. P1 and the other predictions are unchanged.

D2 (2026-11-17, before any gold-scored result; found by counting antecedent-id links, not by scoring). Only MATT, MARK, LUKE and JOHN carry antecedent-id links in greek-nt.xml (ACTS, the epistles and REV carry none: 0 links). The prereg's 8 development books (2COR PHILEM ROM 2TIM JUDE HEB 1PET GAL) therefore have
nothing to score: a dev run on them gave 0 answerable pronouns (read, no result about binding). The same sha256 rule is applied to the four gold-bearing books: first by hash = development: JOHN (4ead...); the other three are the untouched gold test: MATT, MARK, LUKE.
P2-P5, P7, P8 are scored on MATT+MARK+LUKE. The gold-free falsifier P6 keeps the prereg's NT test list (all 16 test books, books with fewer than 20 gendered bindings reported as too thin) and Herodotus. The hold-out registers and the hold-out declension prior excluded all 16 books of the prereg test list, which includes JOHN as well as MATT, MARK, LUKE (stricter than needed: JOHN is development).

D3 (2026-11-17, after dev runs on JOHN only, before any test run; code frozen at the commit that follows). The case-fold as preregistered (a direct pairwise rule) was tried on the development book JOHN and failed in a way the prereg did not foresee: answerable pronouns fell from 402 to 189
because the being of Ἰησοῦς (194 mentions) disappeared. Two causes, both found by printing the cast: (1) "τῷ Ἰησοῦ Σίμων Πέτρος" (Jesus in the dative, then Simon Peter in the nominative) is read as ONE capitalised run, a being nobody is, and a fold that treats Ἰησοῦ and Ἰησοῦς as one token
made Ἰησοῦς a part of it; (2) Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ and Ἰησοῦν Χριστόν were two groups the direct rules could not join (Χριστόν reaches Χριστός and Χριστοῦ reaches Χριστός, not each other), so Ἰησοῦς was ambiguous between two groups and dropped.
Fixes, both from the treebank's own proper nouns and both Greek-only: (a) the prior's `mode: "co-lemma"` (two observed forms are one name when what each can reach under the rules shares a string), (b) the prior's `nameCases` (a form's cases: each counted when at least half its most frequent; the capitalised run breaks between
two names whose case sets are both known and disjoint). REGFOLD and FULL therefore include both. Development numbers seen (JOHN only, stated here so nothing is hidden): REG precision 0.670 coverage 0.694; REGFOLD (hold-out prior) 0.315 and 0.684 with answerable 190 (the hold-out table knows no Gospel name's case, so the break cannot fire and Ἰησοῦς is still lost);
FULL (prior mined from everything) 0.741 and 0.769 with answerable 402. Also changed: P4's false-merge count is reported both ways (by gold entity as preregistered, and by treebank lemma). No dial, threshold or rule was tuned on dev beyond these two repairs; the presence dials are the defaults.

D4 (2026-11-17, after the NT test run, before the Herodotus run). The Herodotus falsifier ran 9 minutes on the first chunks without finishing (the reader is slower than linear on a 168000-character chunk; ACTS at 116000 characters took 75 s). By the prereg's own rule (a book over 5 minutes is cut to its first 60000 characters at a sentence end),
every Herodotus chunk (HDT1, HDT4-HDT8) is cut to its first 60000 characters at a sentence end. The Herodotus result is therefore about 60000 x 6 characters, not the whole of the six chunks. The NT results were not cut.
