# The phasepost formulas — the real ones, with their falsifying controls

*This supersedes the earlier mnemonic version. A "formula" here is the
organ's TYPED SIGNATURE — what it actually returns and what it refuses —
plus its FALSIFYING CONTROL, taken from `native/organs/capacities.js` and
the organs' own tests, never from a gloss. A cell whose organ has no
measured falsifying control is marked **unmeasured** — in this repo that is
a standing, never a pass. The terrain·stance half is `cellOf` and is
omitted where it adds nothing; the ACT is the organ's real behavior. Where
a line names a finding, it names the giver; where it names a refusal, it is
the organ's own string.*

## Existence — NUL · SIG · INS

**NUL·Ground — Void·Clearing — `measure`** (`measure.js::runMeasurement`)
- act: a declared statistic tested against a licensed, deliberately broken null; the gate refuses any statistic/perturbation pair the registry has not licensed.
- control: a statistic insensitive to its perturbation fails invisibly (measured: 1,021 drone flights — a spectrum-preserving null and the declared null gave opposite readings, which is the licensing gate's whole justification). **earned.**

**NUL·Figure — Entity·Dissecting — `clear`** (`clearance.js::makeClearance`)
- act: establishment ladder over a presence set; typed refusal per rung `no_presence` / `below_recurrence_floor` / `ambiguous_surface`; pronoun rung only under declared numbers with a typed skip.
- control: existence ≠ establishment — measured, 84 referents establish and 258 are refused on the Schleswig article; bare "Frederick" EXISTS and is NOT established. **earned.**

**NUL·Pattern — Kind·Unraveling — `kindnull`** (`testKindMembers`)
- act: a DECLARED kind membership challenged against a random-subset binding-energy null; refusals `unknown_members` / `under_powered` / `no_boundary` — a failing set is a verdict with measurement, never a refusal.
- control: the null discriminates, it does not rubber-stamp — measured, Danevirke re-confirmed at p=0.008, a scattered control cleared=false at p=0.264. **earned.**

**SIG·Ground — Void·Tending — `settle`** (`whatWouldSettle`)
- act: name what is absent as the questions that would settle it, ordered by what settles fastest; a search that found nothing is SIGNED as a void, never mistaken for a search never attempted (P32).
- control: measured — a 0.5B reader answered the placement question wrongly; the extent wall refused the answer rather than corrupting the space. **earned.**

**SIG·Figure — Entity·Binding — `cast`** (`makeReferentIndex`)
- act: names resolve to WHO, never to byte strings (P11); `distinguish` is the SIG half of SIG+INS.
- control: the witness tier deliberately stays byte/string-anchored by its own design (P32) — a name resolving to a string rather than a referent breaks the guarantee. **earned.**

**SIG·Pattern — Kind·Tracing — `kinds`** (`projectTerrainInduction`)
- act: recurring kind candidates signed PROVISIONALLY from a population's own interaction field; every basin carries its own null; tiny populations refused `under_powered`.
- control: nothing is promoted by induction alone — induction stays provisional by law. **earned.**

**INS·Ground — Void·Cultivating — `preflight`** (`preflightQuery`)
- act: a materialless grounded turn gets ONE search declared before the model drafts (P23).
- control: none measured — the candidate lives at the fold-architecture boundary, registration waits on it. **unmeasured.**

**INS·Figure — Entity·Making — `cast` + `build`** (`makeBuildLog`)
- act: birth; the INS half of `distinguish` individuates the signed figure; results ATTACH, never re-type.
- control: a capacity's result attaching as a re-type would break the birth's own log (PROPOSE · INS · Figure). **earned.**

**INS·Pattern — Kind·Composing — `skill`** (`runSkilledTask`)
- act: a known procedure instantiated onto new material; zero model calls when a skill claims on its declared anchors; the model's remaining job is slot-filling, one grammar-constrained call.
- control: a skill without a check is refused as a wish. **earned.**

## Structure — SEG · CON · SYN

**SEG·Ground — Field·Clearing — `extent`** (`spaceFrom`)
- act: the extent a question's space must cover, AND ITS UNITS, made operative; `placeFiller` refuses spans the extent cannot contain.
- control: measured — a year-grain extent read "Hamlin (1861–1865)" as complete and could not see Johnson's six weeks inside 1865. **earned.**

**SEG·Figure — Link·Dissecting — `patch`** (`applyOps`)
- act: one `{op:"SEG", find}` snips one span; the act is DERIVED OFF THE BYTES (`deriveOp`/`readOps`), never off a model's label.
- control: measured — both small models say "INS" while supplying a replacement; the cut must come from the bytes. **earned.**

**SEG·Pattern — Network·Unraveling — `unravel`** (`unravel`)
- act: parameter-free separation at the network's bridges; a 2-edge-connected network refused `no_seam` rather than cut with an invented threshold.
- control: a cut bought with an invented threshold — the refusal is the test. **earned.**

**CON·Ground — Field·Tending — `field`** (`advanceSummaryFold`)
- act: append the fold line, count the turn, never decide; the deciding half is separately registered as `atmosphere` (EVA·Ground).
- control: none measured — maintain-vs-judge was gated on the owning session's boundary, not falsified. **unmeasured.**

**CON·Figure — Link·Binding — `relations`** (`makeRelationReader`)
- act: subject–verb–object edges, five typed verdicts `bound` / `contradicted` / `unbound` / `beyond-reach` / `unheard`; every edge span-verified.
- control: measured — 2,584/2,584 edges span-verified against real bytes (P56); an edge not verified breaks the ladder. **earned.**

**CON·Pattern — Network·Tracing — `network`** (`makeNetworkBinder`)
- act: recognizers injected, `RECURRENCE_FLOOR = 2`; a cycle of one shape binds nothing; an unrecognized line is a hole, never a wildcard.
- control: the exemplar cell — a falsifiable prediction stated before the organ existed (a Figure-grain reader on Pattern material returns ZERO edges, not few), confirmed on a real list page, then built. **earned — the strongest control in the map.**

**SYN·Ground — Field·Cultivating — `compile`** (`compilePriors`)
- act: sedimented readings merged into ONE carried ground; compile-once priors with a corpus manifest; pointers with givers, typed gaps for absences, never copies.
- control: compiling never promotes a standing (a law) — no measured falsification run on it. **earned (law), unmeasured (number).**

**SYN·Figure — Link·Making — `hear`** (`makeHyperlexicon`)
- act: a re-sighting folds into the SAME note with witnesses and spans unioned; `admit` returns `turnedAway` and it is not optional.
- control: two pages agreeing becoming two notes (rather than one note, two witnesses) breaks it. **earned.**

**SYN·Pattern — Network·Composing — `graph`** (`chainRelations`)
- act: relations linked to document-order neighbours and referent siblings (↑ before / ↓ after / ⇢ shared cast).
- control: the kernel reaction circuit composes at the same cell but that reading is a NOMINATION, not a documented typing — nothing in the code stamps it. **unmeasured.**

## Interpretation — DEF · EVA · REC

**DEF·Ground — Atmosphere·Clearing — `frame`** (`declareFrame`)
- act: declare the interpretive ambient before the judgment runs; typed refusal `undeclared_frame` naming every missing piece; content-addressed frame ids; the `cross_frame` wall — verdicts from different declared grounds never compare silently.
- control: measured — one suite reading 54/58 under one engine provider and 52/58 under the other was invisible until the frame was declared. **earned.**

**DEF·Figure — Lens·Dissecting — `priors`** (`checkPrior`)
- act: one claim checked against live_priors, provenance-carrying, zero egress; in the void loop, the single cut is cardinality (through `grid.js`).
- control: none measured — the void loop is not yet wired into a live turn (P53's own disclosure); `checkPrior`'s live use is the claim tier over live_priors. **unmeasured (the loop).**

**DEF·Pattern — Paradigm·Unraveling — `declare`** (`acquireCandidates`)
- act: candidate functional/transitive declarations acquired in two tiers — REFUTED and CANDIDATE, never GIVEN.
- control: a corpus can refute a Pattern-grain claim but never earn one (P37, the grain theorem). **earned.**

**EVA·Ground — Atmosphere·Tending — `atmosphere`** (`meterSnapshot`)
- act: a second tier-stack meter over the discourse stream; verdicts CONSUMED as posture and never displayed; the summary-refresh gate (carried when no arrival crossed the null's median).
- control: measured — the safe gate saved 2/9 refresh calls and never went stale; the unsafe one saved 8/9 and shipped a stale topic. **earned.**

**EVA·Figure — Lens·Binding — `web` + `witness`**
- act: `web` = a flagged claim's own words searched, pages judged by the same containment fold, verdicts never stronger than "stated by N of M pages (K distinct hosts)"; `witness` = does one landing actually compile (the EVA every patch passes through).
- control: a verdict phrased stronger than its witnesses. **earned.**

**EVA·Pattern — Paradigm·Tracing — `standing`** (`mergeTestimony`)
- act: AGREE / SINGLE / DISAGREE / CONTRADICTED / UNDETERMINED — a property of the SET that no member carries (P39).
- control: a self-witness (`self:model`) never co-signs corroboration alone; a self-witness opposed by a real refusal is a real disagreement. **earned.**

**REC·Ground — Atmosphere·Cultivating — `regime`** (`atmosphereBoundaries`)
- act: the ambient reading regime's tolerance-triggered re-zero; atmosphere reads at hop = window.
- control: none measured — recourse-locality (regions grow to cover nearly a whole read before conceding) is a NAMED OPEN QUESTION, not a falsified finding. **unmeasured.**

**REC·Figure — Lens·Making — `rezero`** (`rezeroBuild`)
- act: a judged projection's ground conceded and the next ground born; a complaint at a widget IS a re-zero; `grid.js::concedeEvaluation` mirrors it.
- control: a later evaluate that disagrees with an earlier determined verdict must concede it first (P36). **earned.**

**REC·Pattern — Paradigm·Composing — `reshape`** (`reshape`)
- act: a finding contradicting the DECLARED SPACE re-zeros the space; resets the stance ladder, carries testimony, returns extensionally-refused candidates to `wish`.
- control: measured — FDR's space reshaped 1933–1937 → 1933–1945, re-admitted the re-opened filler, then REFUSED to commit ("Henry Wallace" alone is a true sentence and a wrong answer). **earned.**
