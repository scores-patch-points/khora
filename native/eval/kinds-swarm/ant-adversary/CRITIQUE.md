## 1. K1 — company-induced kinds exist (ant-kinds-chat IRC, ant-kinds-ud 19 UD languages)
What the evidence supports: induced kinds are real but COARSE and mostly SYNTACTIC-FRAME (POS-like) classes: function words, aux/modals, base/past/-ing verbs, singular/plural nouns, software/path tokens, greetings+vocatives.
ant-kinds-ud: K* median 3, only the top 2-3 kinds stable across mention floors, no PROPN-majority kind in any of 19 languages (max PROPN share 0.33 hin), its registered stability rule was too permissive
(shuffled control K*=1 at only 86%); amendment A1 (best margin above all 10 shuffled maxima 19/19) is a post-hoc rule. ant-kinds-chat: K1 FAILS its own gap test on IRC (0.03; shuffled company just as stable at small K),
only the transfer ARI 0.47 vs 0.09 supports it. Both: kind labels use the WHOLE stream (the evaluated token's own contexts and future text are inside its kind label) while the reading is causal: a non-causal stratifier.
ATTACK (c), same kind twice (my kind_position.mjs on chat's DEV kindmap and DEV days): the "nick kinds" are POSITION bands. Message-initial share of tokens: kind 9 = 0.759 (248 forms, 12,170 tokens), kind 6 = 0.698,
every other kind <= 0.109, overall 0.095; 91.5% (kind 9) and 88.9% (kind 6) of gold nick occurrences in those kinds are message-initial; kind 6's centroid signature literally starts "before=^".
So "nicks land in ONE induced kind (77% pure, E=16 by their measure)" restates "nicknames are the vocative message-initial words" (the boss's correction, 92.1% initial). It is not a discovered ontological kind;
it is the register's addressing convention, and the chat ant says so itself. Verdict K1: SURVIVES WITH CAVEATS (kinds exist beyond shuffled company, at K 2-3 in UD and via transfer ARI in IRC; the
interesting kinds are position/frame classes, not persons/places; no kind is a proper-noun kind in UD).
Check that would settle it: re-induce with the BOS/EOS features REMOVED (only mid-sentence neighbours) and ask whether a nick kind still forms on IRC and whether PROPN-majority kinds appear in UD with a floor of 3 mentions.

## 4. Other attacks (what each found; (a) confounds are sections 2-3)
(b) LEAKAGE. No gold UPOS, capital or prior enters any reader or any score of ant-kinds-ud (gold UPOS only selects strata and controls: read sig.mjs line 4), ant-kinds-chat (gold = speaker metadata) or my rounds. Three leaks/confounds found: (1) the character-length floor (above);
(2) every kind label is induced on the whole stream/pool including the evaluated token's own contexts and future text (ud: "kind labels selected from the whole DEV stream"; chat: DEV tokens inside the DEV induction pool), a non-causal stratifier (the reading itself is causal);
(3) ant-production reports 85% of cast tokens unseen by the English POS prior vs 100% of controls (coverage, not field) for the production arm.
(c) SAME KIND TWICE. IRC nick kinds 9 and 6 are message-initial bands (0.76 / 0.70 initial vs 0.095). War and Peace's cast kind is the pronoun/determiner subject kind (ant-kinds-novel: 79% of the cast with pronouns and determiners). UD kinds are POS-like (NMI vs UPOS 0.23). So the kinds that exist
are frame classes; "person", "place", "organisation" do not appear except weakly (spa roles/organisations vs places; arb city names vs institutions).
(d) MULTIPLE COMPARISONS. I did not recompute family-wise p-values (no Holm table was produced; the shelved PREREG.md had it). Counting: ant-kinds-ud examined 19 languages x (K1, K2 two nulls, K3 four arms, Simpson in 2 strata) and reports negatives, so the correction can only remove positives;
the positives that matter are ant-kinds-chat's two tier-1 comparisons (DEV +0.155 [0.051, 0.269], CONF +0.084 [0.028, 0.170] over a 6-day block bootstrap; CONF REC+COUNT-COUNT lower bound 0.012): with only 6 blocks and 2 primary tests a Bonferroni 97.5% interval would very likely include 0 on CONF. 
ant-kinds-novel: heterogeneity p = 0.10 / 0.15 (no claim). The Simpson claims: none survive (0 reversals in 19 UD languages; none in chat's nick kinds; chat's modal kind 3 reversal is DEV-only, 15 pairs). ant-shape's register sign flip (names SMALLER shadow in UD, LARGER in IRC) is compatible with, but not shown to be explained by, what I found:
in UD the PROPN signature is not beyond covariates and is at chance after position matching (ant-kinds-ud records), in IRC the record carries a matched signal for nicknames (an addressing convention); extent in tokens favours names 0.64 / 0.73 in my round C (I did not re-test the UD extent sign).
(e) SIMPSON RE-DERIVATION. I did not re-derive per-kind sign reversals myself (my own kind design was shelved). I checked what a conditioning claim needs: on chat's rows the kind-conditioned FIT adds +0.029 [-0.011, 0.064] over the pooled fit (their pooled-days result), in ant-kinds-ud conditioning is -0.077 below the pooled signature,
in ant-shape -0.014: no arm shows conditioning of the MODEL helping; what helps is the kind-matched NEGATIVE set (chat) or the kind LABEL as an additive feature (ud +0.08, shape +0.055).
(f) CROSS-ANT CONSISTENCY. ud kinds (function words / pronoun-aux-verb forms / nominal forms, K 2-3) and chat kinds (24 kinds: function words, base/past/-ing verbs, modals, singular/plural nouns, adjectives, software tokens, greetings, nicknames) agree at the coarse word-class level; chat's finer kinds are enabled by 780k tokens
of IRC against 10-50k-token DEV sets. ant-shape's learned shape is language-general (not family-specific), is carried by magnitude, radius and atmosphere (not by the type profile), is beaten by case-free rivals (0.658 vs 0.605): consistent with the per-kind signatures being mostly size/frequency/position
(ud K2 11/19 beyond frequency-stratified partitions, eta^2 0.03-0.10; chat's kind differences are size effects; novel J4 fails; physics-handles: fragility flat in mention count from 3 mentions, cliff 2.09 at the 2-mention floor). They do not contradict each other.
