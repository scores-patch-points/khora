COMPANY-MODERATORS: pre-registration of candidate moderators (written 2026-10-07, BEFORE any moderator value, descriptor or regression was computed)
=========================================================================================================================================
QUESTION. name-company found a language-general company signal (BOTH@LATER mean AUC 0.615, LEFT@FIRST 0.624) that is very uneven across languages
(fra 0.73 ... tur 0.48). Which language/stream property predicts where it is strong?

DISCLOSURE (what I had seen before writing this). I read NAME-COMPANY-RESULTS.md and printed results/name-company-pairblocks/own.json, so I HAVE SEEN every
language's own-language AUC (LATER LEFT/BOTH, FIRST LEFT/BOTH) and pair counts, and the report's remark that tur/ukr/pol/fin/deu are weak and fra/spa/fas/urd/heb
strong. I noticed while printing that the weak languages mostly have few pairs (ukr 79, jpn 84, rus 110, deu 116, ita 129, tur 140). M1 and M3 below were
suggested by that look; so the directions are NOT blind for these two. They are therefore not allowed to count as confirmed by the dev regression alone: the
rules emitted from this file are confirmed (or killed) only on UD test.conllu, including 28 languages that name-company never touched
(afr bul cat ces cym dan est eus gle glg hrv hun hye kat lav lit lzh mar mlt nob ron slk slv srp tam tel uig wol). I have seen no company AUC and no
descriptor for those 28 languages, and no test.conllu of any language has been opened by anything in this lens.

DEPENDENT VARIABLES (evaluation side only; from own.json of the valid, PAIRBLOCK run; thin languages excluded, as name-company excluded them):
  Y_LATER = BOTH@LATER own-language CV AUC (22 languages); Y_FIRST = LEFT@FIRST own-language CV AUC (23 languages).

THE THREE CANDIDATE MODERATORS (at most three; everything else below is exploratory and gets no regression):
  M1  FWC32  = share of stream tokens whose form is among the 32 commonest forms of that file's own stream (lowercase words, punctuation dropped, as
              readConlluStream; ties broken by form string). STREAM-OBSERVABLE: needs no label, no case, no POS.
              PREDICTED DIRECTION: POSITIVE. Mechanism: the company profile codes each neighbour by its frequency-rank bin; where closed-class words are
              separate high-rank tokens (analytic languages; postpositions in Hindi/Urdu/Persian), a name's left/right neighbours differ in rank-bin from a
              matched open-class word's. Where function is carried by suffixes (tur, fin, hun, pol, rus), the neighbour bins carry little.
  M2  CHAIN  = share of PROPN tokens (gold UPOS, evaluation side) that have an immediately adjacent PROPN in the same sentence of the punctuation-dropped
              stream (multiword names). PREDICTED DIRECTION: POSITIVE. Mechanism: a name inside a name has rare-form neighbours; a matched ordinary word
              rarely does.
  M3  LOGPAIRS = log2(matched pairs available in that stratum), from own.json. A NUISANCE moderator (sample size of the probe; 52 features on 60-600 pairs).
              PREDICTED DIRECTION: POSITIVE. It is not a language property; it is in the model to stop M1/M2 taking credit for training size.

MODELS (fixed now): OLS with intercept. The 7 non-empty subsets of {M1, M2, M3}; features z-scored inside each training fold. Metrics: leave-one-language-out
(LOLO) prediction of each language's AUC; RMSE and MAE of the model vs the mean-only LOLO baseline; skill = 1 - RMSE_model / RMSE_baseline.
  SELECTION CORRECTION: the primary statistic is the BEST skill over the 7 models. Its null is the same best-of-7 statistic recomputed on 2000 permutations of
  the AUC vector against the moderator rows (language-label permutation). A moderator set is SUPPORTED (provisional, dev-only) iff best-of-7 skill >= 0.10, its
  permutation p <= 0.05, and the sign of every coefficient in the winning model equals the predicted sign. A single moderator is called a MODERATOR iff the
  single-moderator model has skill > 0, sign as predicted, and permutation p <= 0.05 (uncorrected, flagged as such).
RESIDUALS: per-language LOLO residual (observed - predicted); OUTLIER if |residual| >= 2 x the SD of LOLO residuals. Approximate sampling SE of each language's
  AUC is reported (Hanley-McNeil with n = pairs) so that residuals can be read against measurement noise.
EXPLORATORY (no inference, no regression; Spearman rho with each AUC and a 2000-permutation p, labelled EXPLORATORY because ~20 descriptors x 2 outcomes
  are scanned): share of PROPN preceded (stream-left neighbour) by ADP/DET/ADJ/NOUN/PROPN/none; share of PROPN sentence-initial; PROPN hapax rate (tokens
  and types); mean PROPN character length; PROPN density; mean sentence length; sentences; LATER/FIRST pairs; cased-script (analysis variable only, share of
  tokens with form != lowercase form); word-order shares from dev gold deprel (nsubj-before-verb, obj-after-verb, case-before-head (prepositions),
  amod-before-noun, det-before-noun) and SOV-like/SVO-like from results/name-shape/_report.json.
EMITTING RULES: at most 2. A rule is emitted only if its moderator set is SUPPORTED or MODERATOR as above; its scope threshold is the moderator value at which
  the dev-fitted model predicts AUC = 0.60, fixed from dev; its confirmation is on UD test.conllu with pass/fail fixed in a SECOND header (the confirmation
  script) BEFORE any test.conllu is opened. If nothing is supported, the null is reported as the finding and no rule is emitted as supported; a partial or
  directional effect is reported as provisional with its numbers.
