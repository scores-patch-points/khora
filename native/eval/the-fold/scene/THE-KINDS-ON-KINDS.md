# Kinds on kinds — the measured phenomenon

*Standing finding, 2026-10-09, after the swarm investigated the 
all-singleton result. One sentence: kinds-on-kinds does not generate a new
level out of nothing — it **telescopes** the first floor, and its behavior is
lifted or blocked by the first floor's own binding, the weld, and the thickness
of the second induction.*

## The discovery path (the ants)

Kinds-on-kinds first returned ALL singletons on every seam — Greek, Sanskrit,
English — and it LOOKED like a deep truth ("induction saturates, the spiral
floor"). The swarm was dispatched. One ant found the bug by construction: the
harness built `company` as a `Map`, and `Object.keys(Map)` is empty, so no pair
was ever compared and every being got its own kind. **Not a degeneracy — a
harness bug.** The organ was fine all along; `scene-kinds.mjs` had used plain
objects and induced real kinds. (The lesson, disclosed: a degenerate result is
a smell — first falsify the measurement, then the world.)

## The phenomenon, once the measure was honest

On the Odyssey (eot-odyssey-521664.min.json, the Greek seam'd read, the
well-bound one):

- **Level 1** (beings → kinds by company, IDF-weighted cosine): 1,160 beings →
  270 kinds at threshold 0.03 — but ONE kind takes 873 members. This is not an
  accident of threshold; as the threshold rises 0.05 → 0.5, the giant contracts
  859 → 485, never vanishing. **The weld is structural:** the Odyssey has a
  transit-hub of beings that co-appear everywhere (the assembly, the council,
  the narrative proem), and single-linkage chaining through hubs welds them.
- **The weld cure** (drop the near-universal seats, present in >40% of scenes):
  783 beings → 206 kinds; the giant shrinks to 539 — still a hub, but the
  marginals are now reading.
- **Level 2** (kinds as beings, their kind-company induced again): 206 kinds →
  188 kinds, and a genuine **19-member second-order kind** emerges — K2 variant
  aggregating K1:1, K1:10, K1:100… — being-kinds that keep the SAME
  *kind-arrangement* across distant scenes. The telescope has appeared: the
  second floor is not a new level, it is the first floor seen through itself.

1. **The second floor is a test of the first.** Kinds-on-kinds adds no meaning
   where the first floor did not bind. English EOT binds 322/16,879 objects
   (2%); its second floor collapsed to singletons — not because induction
   fails, but because **no being keeps real company there**. Greek binds
   5,630/6,824 (83%); its second floor learns. A fold whose second floor is
   blank is reporting a starved seam, not a saturated ontology. Falsifier: a
   seam with well-bound A:/O: seats whose kinds DO NOT lift a second floor —
   if that reads flat, this law is wrong.
2. **The weld is real and must be named, not "fixed away."** A hub-and-spoke
   company graph is a fact about the text (the assembly is genuinely
   everywhere). Removing universal seats is a cure for READABILITY, and must
   be disclosed as such — never hidden as "the kinds."
3. **The telescope is the direction.** The interesting object is the 19-member
   K2: the beings unfolded into a shape the first floor could not show. The
   composition law (grid.js: well-formed moves) holds: INS·Pattern of INS·
   Patterns is a well-formed move; the cube is closed. The second-floor law
   is that it CLOSES — it does not explode.
